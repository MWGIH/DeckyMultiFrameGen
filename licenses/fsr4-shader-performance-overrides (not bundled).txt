fsr4-shader-performance-overrides (Cyboman) and DeckyUpscaler (via the FSR4 timing kit)
======================================================================================

Decky MultiFrameGen does not bundle these binaries. When you pick a Cyboman or Decky FSR 4 option,
the plugin downloads them on your device from:

  https://github.com/zgauthier2000/fsr4-shader-performance-overrides/releases

Cyboman release used: dll-2026-11-05
  Archive: fsr4.1.2-igpu-cyboman-rdna2-2026-11-05.zip (Steam Deck / RDNA2 iGPU)
  Project licence: GNU GPL v2 or later (see that repository's LICENSE)
  The DLLs are modified versions of AMD's amd_fidelityfx_upscaler_dx12.dll; see NOTICE.md in the archive.

Decky (Steamdeck Optimised) uses the DeckyUpscaler v0.2.1 shipped set packaged in:
  timing-kit-2026-11-11 / fsr4-timing-kit-linux-2026-11-11.tar.gz / deckyupscaler/
  DeckyUpscaler is MIT (Copyright (c) 2026 doodlum), included in the timing kit with its author's permission.
  The Vulkan FSR DLL and shader packs remain subject to AMD's FidelityFX SDK licence texts shipped beside them
  in that folder (amd_fidelityfx-LICENSE.md, shaders-LICENSE-AMD.md).

WARNING: Never use these in online games or games with anti-cheat. They are modified game files.
